Adaptations: converted to mono 24 kHz PCM signed 16-bit WAV, capped at 12 seconds
(laughter capped at 3 seconds); most samples use a 35 Hz high-pass. Cue rendering
applies fades, effect-specific RMS gain, soft limiting, and ambience looping. No
source voices are used to train or clone a narrator. Other named effects retain
the automation's locally synthesized fallback; the output sound report identifies
recorded versus synthesized cues. No remote API is used during editing.
